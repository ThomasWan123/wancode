## 4. Post-release maintenance findings

- The production build completed, but the signing wrapper initially passed
  `-p '""'`; the current CLI interpreted the quote characters as a password.
  Signing succeeded with the explicit empty form `--password=`. The wrapper is
  corrected in the post-release maintenance change.
- The first updater E2E safely stopped before running an installer because the
  host's pre-existing uninstall key was absent. The harness now treats absence
  as a valid baseline: it deletes installer-created keys and verifies they
  remain absent. The rerun passed end to end.
- The release-only `wancode-lock-delta` migration mode is restored to steady
  state `equivalent` after the version/dependency delta landed.

## 5. Claims discipline

This release may claim the exact automated gates, signed updater chain, origin
upgrade E2E, and positive mirror transport evidence above. It may not claim
Authenticode trust, full chaos/environment coverage, or superiority over
Claude Code, Codex, or another product. Every `NOT-RUN` remains missing evidence.
