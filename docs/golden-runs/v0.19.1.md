## 1. Decision and scope

  a personal-use product after the stale v0.19.0 release was withdrawn.
- The updater trust boundary remains the pinned minisign key. Absence of
  Authenticode is disclosed and is not rewritten as a passing trust check.
- Authenticode reopens before public promotion, external-user distribution,
  marketplace/store delivery, or deployment into strict-EDR environments.
- The release commit changes only the version manifests and locked WanCode
  package version. Engine identity and effective-tree bindings remain explicit.
